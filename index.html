How India’s ask can travel

Make the
case
travel.

No website can promise access to Abel Tesfaye. This is the credible, respectful route to give an India show proposal a chance to be seen.

01

Collect real, consented demand.

Maintain a secure, India-only form. Publish only aggregate results and never individual names or emails.

02

Turn it into a promoter brief.

Share city demand, likely ticket ranges, campaign reach and audience quality only as aggregates. A recognised India concert promoter can then evaluate venue, production and routing feasibility.

03

Let the promoter approach the booking side.

A professional promoter—not random DMs—should make the formal approach to the artist’s authorised live and booking representatives with a clear proposal.

04

Use public channels with respect.

Publish one clear monthly campaign update, invite fans to share it, and tag only verified official accounts. Never pretend to be official, mass-message the artist, or promise a concert.

The right lane

Reach the team, not the noise.

Official social accounts and the mailing list are useful places to follow releases and share a public campaign update. They are not a promise of direct contact or a booking channel.
