> **"If 80,000 voices can sell out Coldplay and Dua Lipa in India, why not Abel Tesfaye?"**

An independent, 100% free and non-commercial fan-led initiative to centralize, verify, and demonstrate massive stadium demand for **The Weeknd (Abel Tesfaye)** across India.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Mission

India has rapidly become one of the premier live entertainment and stadium concert destinations in the world. Despite Abel Tesfaye being one of the most streamed international artists in the subcontinent, India is frequently overlooked during initial global stadium tour routing due to a lack of centralized, verifiable demand analytics.

**theweeknd.co.in** was engineered to solve this:
1. **Verified City-Wise Voting:** Fans across 15+ cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, etc.) register their demand.
2. **Anti-Scalper & Transparent:** Zero ticket reselling, zero commercial transactions, 100% pure fan data.
3. **Official Dossier Presentation:** Aggregate city demand and demographic metrics to present directly to Abel's global management, Live Nation India, and BookMyShow Live.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Responsive Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), DM Mono & Montserrat Typography.
- **Database & Realtime Analytics:** Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security & real-time listeners).
- **Backend & Serverless:** Vercel Edge Serverless Functions.
- **Transactional Delivery:** Resend API (`campaign@theweeknd.co.in` via custom authenticated DNS).
- **Video & Reel Mastering:** FFmpeg 5.0 pipeline with custom 35mm film grain, rising crimson atmosphere, and lossless AAC 320k audio encoding.

---

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

- **Zero Hardcoded Secrets:** All transactional dispatch is handled via isolated serverless functions with protected environment variables.
- **Privacy-First Fan Verification:** Email addresses and voter metadata are encrypted and never sold, shared with third-party advertisers, or monetized.
